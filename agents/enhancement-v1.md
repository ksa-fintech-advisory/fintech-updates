# Business Context
This is for "Mal Tech" (مال تك), the professional platform for Mohammed Abdo, a Senior Fintech Software Architect with 5+ years of experience. 
The platform is NOT a standard freelancer portfolio. It is a High-End Engineering Consulting Hub. It bridges the critical gap between complex financial regulatory compliance (e.g., CMA, SAMA) and actual software implementation.

# Target Audiences & Offerings
The platform serves two distinct user journeys:
1. B2B (Startup Founders, CTOs, Angel Investors): 
   - Pain point: They need to launch Fintech MVPs rapidly without failing regulatory compliance or accumulating technical debt.
   - Services offered: Architecture Tear-downs, Pre-Build Compliance Audits, Fractional Architect consulting, and bridging the gap between legal requirements and code.
2. B2C/Professionals (Software Engineers & Tech Leads): 
   - Pain point: They need to upgrade their careers to understand complex Fintech architecture and compliance.
   - Services offered: Specialized Fintech engineering roadmaps, technical guidance, and advanced architecture cohorts.

# Your Mission
Audit the current workflow, accessibility, and user journeys of the platform. Propose a highly simplified, intuitive, and premium UX/UI strategy that separates the B2B journey from the B2C journey without confusing the user.

- 100% Human-Centric Output: Do not use AI-generated cliches, buzzwords, or robotic marketing fluff (e.g., avoid words like "seamless," "robust," "delve," "synergy").
